# Apple — Human Interface Guidelines: Typography

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

"Typography," *Apple Human Interface Guidelines* (Apple Developer Documentation), living platform documentation, **live page read this session**. The platform-vendor prescription artifact for small-screen and multi-platform typography: a per-platform **default and minimum text-size table** (iOS/iPadOS default **17 pt**, minimum **11 pt**; macOS 13/10 pt; tvOS 29/23 pt; visionOS 17/12 pt; watchOS 16/12 pt), weight guidance ("avoid light font weights" at small sizes), legibility-testing guidance, Dynamic Type size tables, and tracking tables.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **primary artifact of the practice being studied**: the most widely applied small-screen typography prescription set, admitted to this base **DELIBERATELY as the platform-vendor (i)/(ii) pole ONLY** — its role is to state the vendor's prescriptions in their prescribed form, **not to evidence them**.

**Citation-trail record (executed at cataloguing):** the page was **live-read this session**. Its prescriptive statements ("Use font sizes that most people can read easily," the per-platform minimum-size table, the light-weight avoidance rule) are stated without any cited study, measurement, or research reference anywhere on the page; the page's justificatory register is testing guidance ("Test legibility in different contexts"), not cited evidence. **The trail terminates in assertion at the artifact itself, at the surface read this session.**

**Stake note (descriptive):** Apple is the platform's commercial owner; the guidelines prescribe use of its own system fonts and type system. Recorded as a fact about provenance, not as an assessment.
