# W3C — Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

*Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1*, **W3C Recommendation** (first published **5 June 2018**; the live TR page read this session carries the **06 May 2025** revised edition), editors Andrew Kirkpatrick (Adobe), Joshue O Connor (Invited Expert), Alastair Campbell (Nomensa), Michael Cooper (W3C). The **normative statement of the luminance-contrast thresholds** this investigation interrogates: Success Criterion **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)** — text contrast ratio **at least 4.5:1**, with **3:1 for large-scale text** (defined as at least 18 point, or 14 point bold, with a CJK-equivalence clause) — plus SC 1.4.6 (enhanced, 7:1) and the contrast-ratio formula defined in the document's own terms.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **primary artifact of the practice being studied**: the world's most widely adopted (and widely legislated) text-contrast prescription, admitted to this base as the **prescription pole for the size/contrast-threshold territory** — its normative statements are the (i)/(ii) object under examination. The provenance of its thresholds and formula (what measurement, if any, they rest on — the accompanying *Understanding* documents cite prior standards lineage such as ISO 9241 and ANSI/HFES work) is exactly the question the investigation examines; **nothing about that provenance is asserted or assessed here.** (What kind of document it is and what it is about — no assessment is made or implied.)

# 3. Limitations at cataloguing

- The TR page was **partially live-read this session** (SC 1.4.3/1.4.6 thresholds, large-text definition, editors, dates); the full document — in particular the *Understanding SC 1.4.3* companion and its citation trail — was **not read at cataloguing** and is circuit work. No claim about the thresholds' provenance is made here.
- Version note: WCAG 2.1's TR page carries in-place revised editions; the edition read is disclosed above (06 May 2025). The 4.5:1 / 3:1 thresholds are continuous with the 2008 WCAG 2.0 Recommendation; that lineage is recorded as lineage, not assessed.
