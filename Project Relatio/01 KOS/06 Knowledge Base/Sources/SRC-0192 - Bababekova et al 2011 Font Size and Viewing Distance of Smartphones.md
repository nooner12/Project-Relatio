# Bababekova, Rosenfield, Hue & Huang (2011) — Font Size and Viewing Distance of Handheld Smart Phones

# 1. Identification

Yuliya Bababekova, Mark Rosenfield, Jennifer E. Hue, and Rae R. Huang, "Font size and viewing distance of handheld smart phones," *Optometry and Vision Science*, **88(7), 795–797 (2011)**, **DOI 10.1097/OPX.0b013e3182198792**. The small-screen territory's direct measurement of **actual viewing behavior**: font sizes and working distances measured while subjects used handheld devices — reported at the surface as mean working distances of **36.2 cm for text messages and 32.2 cm for internet viewing**, closer than the conventional 40 cm near-work distance that print-size prescriptions typically assume.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
