# Jonauskaite et al. (2020) — Universal Patterns in Color-Emotion Associations Are Further Shaped by Linguistic and Geographic Proximity

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Domicele Jonauskaite, Ahmad Abu-Akel, Nele Dael, Daniel Oberfeld, and colleagues (34 authors; Christine Mohr senior author), "Universal patterns in color-emotion associations are further shaped by linguistic and geographic proximity," *Psychological Science*, **31(10), 1245–1260 (2020)**, **DOI 10.1177/0956797620948810**. A large cross-national survey study of color-emotion **associations**: 4,598 participants, 30 nations, 22 native languages, 12 color terms × 20 emotion concepts.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the brand-visual-design response-evidence investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

An **empirical survey study** measuring which emotions people across nations *associate* with color terms, and how similarity in those associations varies with linguistic and geographic proximity. It is the base's principal cross-cultural measurement of the color-emotion association family. Its subject is stated associations with color **terms** (words), a distinct object from affective or behavioral **response** to presented color. (Descriptive characterisation only; no assessment of quality or authority.)
