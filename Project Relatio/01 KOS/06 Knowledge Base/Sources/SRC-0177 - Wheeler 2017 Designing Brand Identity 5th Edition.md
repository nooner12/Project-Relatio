# 1. Identification

Alina Wheeler, *Designing Brand Identity: An Essential Guide for the Whole Branding Team*, **5th edition, Wiley, 2017, ISBN 978-1-118-98082-8** (336 pp.). A standard practitioner manual of brand-identity practice (strategy, design execution, launch, governance), published in 11 languages; a 6th edition (2024, with Rob Meyerson) exists and is noted as context — **this record catalogs the 5th edition specifically** (edition discipline).

Catalogued as a **source base record for the brand-visual-design response-evidence investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **practitioner brand-identity manual**, admitted to this base **DELIBERATELY as the practitioner (i)/(ii) pole ONLY**: its role is to state what the brand-design practice literature prescribes and the rationales it gives — **not to evidence those prescriptions**. Nothing it asserts populates any measured-evidence element anywhere in the investigation.

**Stake note (descriptive):** the author is a professional brand consultant and the book is commercial professional guidance; the commercial design industry has a stake in its prescriptions being regarded as effective. Recorded as a fact about provenance, not as an assessment.
