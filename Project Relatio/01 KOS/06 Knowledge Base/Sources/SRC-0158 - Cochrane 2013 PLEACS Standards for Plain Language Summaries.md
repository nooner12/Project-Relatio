# 1. Identification

*Standards for the reporting of Plain Language Summaries in new Cochrane Intervention Reviews* (**PLEACS**), Cochrane, **2013** (version 3.0, dated 28 February 2013). An **organizational reporting standard** (no individual authors listed) prescribing how a Cochrane plain-language summary is to be written and structured — an **expert-to-lay register** standard.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the INV-0019 prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **reporting-standards document** setting the required content and form of plain-language summaries for new Cochrane intervention reviews. It is about **translating systematic-review findings into a lay register**. (Descriptive characterisation only; no quality or authority assessment.)

# 3. Edition determination (EDITION DISCIPLINE — brief's third open question, RESOLVED)

**This record catalogs the 2013 PLEACS standard — NOT the current "Guidance for writing a Cochrane Plain language summary" (Cochrane Handbook, Chapter III supplementary material).** The two are **different documents of different vintage** and are **not** blended here. **Reasoning, recorded:**
- **SRC-0159 (Jelicic Kadic et al. 2016) is the empirical adherence study OF this standard** (the coverage pairing 7↔8), and it measured adherence **specifically against PLEACS** — its own reference 2 cites *"Standards for the reporting of Plain Language Summaries in new Cochrane Intervention Reviews (PLEACS)"* (2013, v3.0). Cataloguing record 7 as the later Handbook Chapter III guidance would make the SRC-0159 → SRC-0158 relationship **false**, because Kadic did not measure the Handbook guidance.
- PLEACS is therefore the **load-bearing** choice: it is the exact standard against which the base's adherence study was conducted.
- The current Handbook Chapter III **"Guidance for writing a Cochrane Plain language summary"** is a **different, later document** and is **deliberately not catalogued** in this base.
