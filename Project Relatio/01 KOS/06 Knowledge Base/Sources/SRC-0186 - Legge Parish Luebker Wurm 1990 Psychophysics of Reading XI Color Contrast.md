# Legge, Parish, Luebker & Wurm (1990) — Psychophysics of Reading XI: Comparing Color Contrast and Luminance Contrast

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Gordon E. Legge, David H. Parish, Andrew Luebker, and Lee H. Wurm, "Psychophysics of reading. XI. Comparing color contrast and luminance contrast," *Journal of the Optical Society of America A*, **7(10), 2002–2010 (1990)**, **PMID 2231110**. The series' direct measurement of reading with **chromatically defined text**: reading speeds of normal and low-vision subjects for text defined by luminance contrast, by color contrast (red/green mixtures), and by combinations — reported at the surface as finding high color contrast supporting reading **as rapid as high luminance contrast** (>300 wpm for normal subjects), with matching curve shapes when contrast is expressed in threshold multiples and no additive interaction between the two contrast forms.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
