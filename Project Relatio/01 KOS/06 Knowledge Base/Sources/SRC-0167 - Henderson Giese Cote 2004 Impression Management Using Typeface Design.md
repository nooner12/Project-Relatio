# 1. Identification

Pamela W. Henderson, Joan L. Giese, and Joseph A. Cote, "Impression management using typeface design," *Journal of Marketing*, **68(4), 60–72 (2004)**, **DOI 10.1509/jmkg.68.4.60.42736**. An empirical study of the impressions typefaces create (reported design: 210 typefaces rated on 24 design characteristics, reduced to six underlying design dimensions), producing empirically based typeface-selection guidance for managers.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the brand-visual-design response-evidence investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
