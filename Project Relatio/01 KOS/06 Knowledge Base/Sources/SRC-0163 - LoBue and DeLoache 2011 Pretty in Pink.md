# LoBue & DeLoache (2011) — Pretty in Pink: The Early Development of Gender-Stereotyped Colour Preferences

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Vanessa LoBue and Judy S. DeLoache, "Pretty in pink: The early development of gender-stereotyped colour preferences," *British Journal of Developmental Psychology*, **29(3), 656–667 (2011)**, **DOI 10.1111/j.2044-835X.2011.02027.x**. A cross-sectional behavioral-choice study of children aged 7 months to 5 years (paired-object forced choice, one pink object per pair) on the developmental emergence of gender-stereotyped color preference.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the brand-visual-design response-evidence investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

An **empirical developmental study**: measured object-choice behavior across age bands, bearing on whether color preference is developmentally stable or emerges with age (the study's reported design places preference emergence around age 2–2.5 rather than in infancy). It is the base's direct measurement instrument for the **age / developmental-stage** variation sub-element. (Descriptive characterisation only; no assessment of quality or authority.)

# 3. Limitations at cataloguing

- **Interior parametric / unread** at cataloguing; the characterisation above is recorded from the bibliographic surface and abstract. No page-level claim is drawn.
- The study concerns **preference for pink** in young children; it is not a study of branding, marketing exposure, or hue-response generally. Its bearing on any claim is decided at circuit, not here.
