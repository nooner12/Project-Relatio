# 1. Identification

Andrew Dillon, "Reading from paper versus screens: a critical review of the empirical literature," *Ergonomics*, **35(10), 1297–1326 (1992)**, **DOI 10.1080/00140139208967394**. The medium-divergence literature's foundational critical review: reported at the surface as finding the early literature dominated by speed deficits attributable to poor image quality, while arguing that **single-variable explanations are insufficient** to capture the outcome and process differences between media.
