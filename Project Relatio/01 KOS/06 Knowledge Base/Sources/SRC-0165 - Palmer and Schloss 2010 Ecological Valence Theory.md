# 1. Identification

Schloss, "An ecological valence theory of human color preference," *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, **107(19), 8877–8882 (2010)**, **DOI 10.1073/pnas.0906172107**. An empirical and theoretical treatment of color **preference** — the theory that people like colors to the degree they like the objects associated with those colors.
