# 1. Identification

Yuwei Jiang, Gerald J. Gorn, Maria Galli, and Amitava Chattopadhyay, "Does your company have the right logo? How and why circular- and angular-logo shapes influence brand attribute judgments," *Journal of Consumer Research*, **42(5), 709–726 (2016)**, **DOI 10.1093/jcr/ucv049**. A five-experiment study of logo **roundness vs. angularity**: circular vs. angular shapes reported as activating softness vs. hardness associations that carry into product/company attribute judgments.
