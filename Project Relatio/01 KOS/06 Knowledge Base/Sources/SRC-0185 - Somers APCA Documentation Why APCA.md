# 1. Identification

The **APCA (Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm) documentation** authored by Andrew Somers (Myndex Research): principally "Why APCA as a New Contrast Method?" and the SAPC-APCA repository documentation, live pages as of this session. APCA is the candidate readability-contrast model developed in connection with the **WCAG 3 exploratory work**; its documentation states the criticisms of the WCAG 2.x contrast method this investigation examines — including claimed **perceptual non-uniformity** of the 2.x ratio, claimed **polarity insensitivity** (light-on-dark vs dark-on-light), and claimed inattention to **font weight and spatial frequency** — and describes the replacement model (Lc values, polarity-aware, weight-aware).

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
