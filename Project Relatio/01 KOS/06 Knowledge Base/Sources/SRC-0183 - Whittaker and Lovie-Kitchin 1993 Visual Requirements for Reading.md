# 1. Identification

Stephen G. Whittaker and Jan Lovie-Kitchin, "Visual requirements for reading," *Optometry and Vision Science*, **70(1), 54–65 (1993)**, **PMID 8430009**. The reserve-model synthesis of the reading-requirements literature: reading rate as a function of **acuity reserve** (print size relative to acuity threshold), **contrast reserve** (print contrast relative to contrast threshold), field of view, and central scotoma size — reported at the surface with quantitative reserve thresholds for different reading-fluency levels (e.g., a contrast reserve of at least 10:1 associated with fluent-range reading, lower reserves with spot reading).

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
