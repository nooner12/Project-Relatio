# Butterick — Practical Typography (Summary of Key Rules)

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Matthew Butterick, *Butterick's Practical Typography* (2nd edition, web book), principally its "Summary of key rules" page, **live-read this session**. A widely circulated professional typography prescription set — the page states, among others: body point size **10–12 points in print, 15–25 pixels on the web**; **line spacing 120–145%** of point size; **line length 45–90 characters** — the practitioner-consensus statements of exactly the prescriptions whose evidential status this investigation examines.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **primary artifact of the practice being studied**: a professional practitioner's prescription book, admitted to this base **DELIBERATELY as the practitioner (i)/(ii) pole ONLY** — its role is to state the professional-consensus rules in their circulated form, **not to evidence them**.

**Citation-trail record (executed at cataloguing):** the summary page was **live-read this session**. Its rules are stated **without cited research, studies, footnotes, or references** on that page; the rules are presented as professional principles. (The book's other pages may discuss provenance; only the summary surface is recorded here, and the limitation is disclosed.)

**Stake note (descriptive):** the author is a practicing typographer and type designer who sells fonts and the book itself; the book prescribes practices adjacent to his commercial work. Recorded as a fact about provenance, not as an assessment.

# 3. Limitations at cataloguing
