# Lund (1999) — Knowledge Construction in Typography: The Case of Legibility Research and the Legibility of Sans Serif Typefaces

# 1. Identification

Ole Lund, *Knowledge construction in typography: the case of legibility research and the legibility of sans serif typefaces*, PhD thesis, Department of Typography & Graphic Communication, **The University of Reading, 1999**. A historiographic and epistemological examination of the legibility-research tradition, reported at the surface as a critical review of **72 typeface-comparison studies** concluding that **no valid conclusion in favour of either serif or sans-serif typefaces** can be drawn from that research lineage — the literature's principal internal critique of how typographic legibility knowledge was constructed.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **doctoral thesis at the field's principal typographic research department**, examining the methodological quality and epistemic status of the serif/sans-serif legibility research corpus rather than adding a new measurement. Its role in this base is as documentation of **what the prescriptive dispute's evidence lineage actually contains** — a boundary-locating document for the measured/asserted distinction this investigation examines. (What kind of document it is and what it is about — no assessment of quality, strength, or authority is made or implied.)
