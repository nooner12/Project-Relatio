# Legge, Rubin & Luebker (1987) — Psychophysics of Reading V: The Role of Contrast in Normal Vision

## Draft Source Record

# 1. Identification

Gordon E. Legge, Gary S. Rubin, and Andrew Luebker, "Psychophysics of reading — V. The role of contrast in normal vision," *Vision Research*, **27(7), 1165–1177 (1987)**. The reading-contrast measurement paper of the psychophysics-of-reading series: reading rate measured as a function of text contrast and character size in normal vision, reported at the surface as finding reading **highly tolerant of contrast reduction** over a wide range (for 1° letters, less than a twofold rate decrease for a tenfold contrast reduction), with similar results for light-on-dark and dark-on-light text.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
