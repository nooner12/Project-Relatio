# 1. Identification

Gabrielle K. Lehmann and Robert J. Calin-Jageman, "Is red really romantic? Two pre-registered replications of the red-romance hypothesis," *Social Psychology*, **48(3), 174–183 (2017)**, **DOI 10.1027/1864-9335/a000296**. Two pre-registered replications (online and in-person) of the red-romance effect, using original materials, planned sample sizes, a positive control, and quality controls; reported results were little-to-no effect (men d ≈ 0.09; women d ≈ −0.09).

Catalogued as a **source base record for the brand-visual-design response-evidence investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
