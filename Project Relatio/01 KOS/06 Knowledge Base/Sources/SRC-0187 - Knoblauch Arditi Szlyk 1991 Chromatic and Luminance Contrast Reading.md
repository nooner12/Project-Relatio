# Knoblauch, Arditi & Szlyk (1991) — Effects of Chromatic and Luminance Contrast on Reading

# 1. Identification

Kenneth Knoblauch, Aries Arditi, and Janet Szlyk, "Effects of chromatic and luminance contrast on reading," *Journal of the Optical Society of America A*, **8(2), 428–439 (1991)**, **PMID 2007918**. The Lighthouse laboratory's measurement of reading for text defined by chromatic contrast with varying luminance contrast — reported at the surface as finding that with modest luminance contrast present, chromatic contrast neither helped nor hurt across a 30-fold character-size range, while near the luminance-contrast reading threshold, **chromatic contrast alone sustained reading rates near 300 words per minute**.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
