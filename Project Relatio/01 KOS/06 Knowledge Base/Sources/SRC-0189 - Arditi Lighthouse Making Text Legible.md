# SRC-0189

# Arditi / Lighthouse — Making Text Legible: Designing for People with Partial Sight

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Aries Arditi, *Making Text Legible: Designing for People with Partial Sight*, Lighthouse International guideline brochure (c. 1995; undated editions circulate, and the successor organization Lighthouse Guild maintains a version). A short **design-prescription artifact** stating legibility guidelines for partially sighted and older readers — reported at the surface as prescribing maximum contrast (with a stated preference claim for light-on-dark for many older/partially sighted readers), leading of at least 25–30% of point size, avoidance of complicated/decorative fonts, non-glossy paper, and related choices.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

A **prescription artifact authored by a vision scientist** whose own laboratory produced part of the measurement literature in this base (SRC-0180, SRC-0187) — the base's clearest case of a **derived prescription**: guidance that claims descent from measurement. Whether the derivations match what was measured is exactly the CLM-D question; **nothing about that match is asserted or assessed here.** Admitted as the **(i)/(ii) prescription pole for the aging/partial-sight territory.** (What kind of document it is and what it is about — no assessment is made or implied.)

**Stake note (descriptive):** authored by the researcher whose measurement literature it draws on — a prescription and its evidential base sharing an author; Lighthouse is a vision-services organization. Recorded as facts about provenance, not as an assessment.
