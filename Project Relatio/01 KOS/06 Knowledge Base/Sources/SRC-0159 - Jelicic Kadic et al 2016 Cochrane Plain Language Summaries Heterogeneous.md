# Jelicic Kadic et al. (2016) — Cochrane plain language summaries are highly heterogeneous

## Draft Source Record

---

# 1. Identification

Antonia Jelicic Kadic, Mahir Fidahic, Milan Vujcic, Frano Saric, Ivana Propadalo, Ivana Marelja, Svjetlana Dosenovic, and Livia Puljak, "Cochrane plain language summaries are highly heterogeneous with low adherence to the standards," *BMC Medical Research Methodology*, **16:61 (2016)**, **DOI 10.1186/s12874-016-0162-y** (PMID 27216616). An **empirical study of the adherence** of Cochrane plain-language summaries to the **PLEACS** reporting standard (SRC-0158).

Catalogued as a **source base record for the INV-0019 prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**

# 2. Description (scope note — descriptive only)

An **empirical adherence study** analysing whether Cochrane plain-language summaries conform to the PLEACS reporting standard, across a large sample of summaries. It is about **measured adherence of a lay-register writing standard in practice**. (Descriptive characterisation only; the study's own findings are not restated as INV-0019 findings and no quality judgement of the source is made.)

# 3. Limitations at cataloguing

- **Interior parametric / unread** this session; the study's scope is recorded from its abstract and its PMC record, not from a reading of the interior. No page-level claim (including its numerical adherence results) is drawn into this catalog record as an INV-0019 finding.

# 4. Relationships (STD-0004)

- `related_to` **SRC-0158** (PLEACS 2013) — **reciprocated.** This study **empirically evaluates the adherence** of Cochrane plain-language summaries to the **PLEACS** standard; its own reference 2 cites PLEACS directly, which is also what fixes SRC-0158's edition (see SRC-0158 §3).
- **Relationship-type gap (routed, not forced).** The true relationship here is *"empirically evaluates adherence/compliance with the standard in SRC-0158."* **None of the approved STD-0004 types honestly fits** it: it is not `derived_from` (the study is not derived from the standard), not `depends_on` (the KB sense would misstate the standard's validity as contingent), not `supports` (it reports *low* adherence *to* the standard, not support of it), not `contrasts_with` (it is a measurement, not a rival position), not `explains`/`extends`/`implements`. This is precisely the case the brief names — *"evaluates the compliance of" is not obviously any of the approved types.* Per Task 3, **no type was forced and none minted.** The genuine connection is recorded with the honest weak type `related_to`, and the **precise adherence-evaluation type is routed to the Governance Backlog as a candidate** (GB item recorded this session). A candidate name is offered there, not adopted here.
