# Arditi & Cho (2005) — Serifs and Font Legibility

## Draft Source Record

# 1. Identification

Aries Arditi and Jianna Cho, "Serifs and font legibility," *Vision Research*, **45(23), 2926–2933 (2005)**, **DOI 10.1016/j.visres.2005.06.013**. A controlled measurement study of the serif question using a specially designed parametric font whose variants differ only in serif presence, reported at the surface as finding **no significant reading-speed differences** between serif and sans-serif variants (RSVP and paper presentation) alongside measured preference results — a direct experimental test of one of typography's oldest prescriptive disputes.

Catalogued as a **source base record for the reading-performance investigation prep** under the **ADR-GOV-0003 pattern** (catalog only, grade nothing). **No investigation is opened, no claim recorded, no grade assigned.**
