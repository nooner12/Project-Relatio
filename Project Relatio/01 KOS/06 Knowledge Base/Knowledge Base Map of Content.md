# 1. Purpose

This is the Knowledge Base's front door: a topical map for someone who has a question and wants to know whether this vault already addresses it, and if so which record to open.

It is written for a **contributor who asks questions** — not for the owner, who knows what is here, and not for tooling, which reads frontmatter. It groups the nineteen investigations by subject area, says in a line or two what each one asked and what it found, and sends the reader to the record. Everything below is a signpost. The records are the knowledge.

---

# 2. What this document is not

- **It is not an Index.** STD-0003 §12 defines an Index as an authoritative registry answering *"what exists"*; §13 defines a Map of Content as an interpretive structure answering *"how should this domain be explored."* This is the §13 object. It answers the second question and has no authority to answer the first.
- **It is not an inventory, and no inventory is implied.** The §12 generated inventory of Knowledge Base objects is **deliberately deferred** by owner decision. There is no per-object census here — no claim list, no source list, no entity list, no counts to reconcile. For "what exists," use the **Identifier Registry**.
- **It may lag the vault, and that is acceptable for this object class.** An interpretive map that is slightly behind is still doing its job; an authoritative registry that is behind is lying. Clusters are a reading aid, not a classification with consequences.
- **The records are authoritative.** Where this document and a record disagree, **the record wins** — and the divergence is worth reporting.
- **It re-grades nothing.** Confidence and reliance shown here are quoted from the records at the strength the records carry. Nothing is averaged across claims, nothing is restated more strongly, and no new grade is assigned anywhere.

# 3. The reliance gate

**Everything in this Knowledge Base is `R0`. Nothing is cleared for external reliance.**

- `R0` is the unverified floor of the three-tier model (ADR-GOV-0006): R0 parametric · R1 retrieval-verified under edition discipline · R2 independently verified. No object in this vault has reached R1 or R2.
- **Closure does not clear anything.** Every closed investigation states this on its own record.
- **Some records are gated further, and the further gates come in three kinds.**
  - **Health / high-stakes and child-facing (STD-0006 §7.5).** These records are non-citable in client-facing, prospect-facing, or public material in any stream, at any confidence level, pending re-verification outside Relatio's own circuit.
  - **Externally consequential (STD-0006 §7.5, the same rule reaching further).** §7.5 governs the *strength* of a review's own check: a verification-light review may issue verdicts but **may not clear an object for external reliance**, and the standard says the rule is "especially load-bearing for health, safety, financial, or **otherwise externally-consequential** findings." A record can therefore be gated without being health or child-facing. **INV-0014 is the plain case** and states it on its own face — not health-gated, not child-facing, but externally consequential because it sits adjacent to the owner's comparative-religion thesis; its findings are **not cleared for external reliance (thesis or public use) regardless of closure**, and genuinely independent re-verification is the gate-lift. Several other records in the religion, texts, and tradition-history clusters carry the same §7.5-analog note. Read the record.
  - **Reflexive (STD-0006 §7.6).** Reflexive material may never be cited as vindication of this vault's own design, governance, or method. The gate lifts only on blinded independent review.
- **Every citation out of Relatio goes through STD-0006 Appendix A.** That crosswalk is the only authorized native→house translation, it is applied **at the boundary on the way out**, and it **maps conservatively**. The four-tier house scale is external vocabulary and does not appear on any Knowledge Object; records use native `Level N (Label)` only.
- **Translation never clears a gate, and a finding never gains strength in translation.** A gate travels with the citation. If a record is gated, the citation is gated.
- **Independence is assessed at family level.** Same model family is same-kind regardless of version or operator, so a Claude-family review of Claude-family work is not independence of kind (ADR-GOV-0011). Most of this corpus was produced and reviewed inside one family; that is why the gates stand.

# 4. Domain clusters

Clusters, derived from reading the records. They are groupings for a reader, nothing more — no record's meaning depends on which cluster it sits in.

One honest caveat about the shape: **the corpus does not cluster evenly.** Nine of the nineteen investigations concern religion, texts, and tradition history, and they do not form one subject — they split into three genuinely different kinds of question. The remaining ten spread across four areas: one holds four records, the other three hold two apiece. A tidy scheme would have been less true to what is here.

## 4.1 Comparative teachings and concepts

*What this cluster answers:* what a teaching or a concept **means**, and how two traditions' teachings compare when read as texts. These are interpretive questions about meaning, not historical questions about events or descent. Both records bracket historicity, both name translation loss as a live risk, and both cap their integrated interpretation at Moderate because an interpretive synthesis is a defensible reading rather than a fact.

**Neither record carries an owner's-thesis posture, and that absence is chronological, not a clearance.** Both were authored 2026-07-09, eleven days before that discipline first appears (INV-0014 and INV-0015, both 2026-07-20). INV-0001 in particular reads two traditions side by side on the owner's own comparative ground, and is the record here most likely to be reached for in a thesis's service. It sits under the same general R0 gate as everything else in this Knowledge Base — **not cleared for external reliance, and specifically not for supporting a thesis** (§3). Throughout this document, the absence of a named thesis gate means the practice postdates the record, never that the record is cleared.

**INV-0001 — Comparative Teachings of Jesus and Philosophical Daoism**
Asked: on two selected themes, how do the teachings attributed to Jesus in the canonical Gospels compare with those of philosophical Daoism — one apparent convergence, one substantive divergence, with the interpretive risk of each.
Found: a striking resonance in an ethic of non-striving resting on **opposed conceptions of ultimate reality**. The record is explicit that "the similarity is genuine; the identity is not" — the divergence is what stops the convergence from amounting to sameness. The two halves are graded separately and unequally: the **non-striving convergence is Level 3 (Moderate)**, the **ultimate-reality divergence is Level 4 (High)**, and the integrated finding is capped at **Level 3 (Moderate)**. The weight sits on the stronger half. Claims are about the teachings *as the texts present them*; historicity is bracketed.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0003 — Wu Wei**
Asked: what *wu wei* (無為) means, and how its central paradox — action through non-action — should be understood across its major interpretations.
Found: not passivity but non-coercive, effortless action in accord with *ziran*; the paradox dissolves once *wu wei* is read as the absence of forced, self-conscious striving rather than the absence of activity — **Level 4 (High)** for that core reading. Its second half, that the political, self-cultivational, and metaphysical registers express **one** ideal, is **Level 3 (Moderate)** and the record calls the unified framing "defensible but contested." The integrated finding is **Level 3 (Moderate)**. Do not read the unification as settled.
Closed 2026-07-20.

## 4.2 Textual and historical evidence for Jesus and the earliest movement

*What this cluster answers:* what the sources are and what they can carry — dating, authorship, transmission, and what can be established about a historical figure and the stream he stood in. These three records form a deliberate chain: INV-0013 grades the source base, INV-0014 builds on those layer grades by citation rather than re-derivation, and INV-0011 traces what the movement inherited from upstream.

**A bracket here, and it must not be confused with a grade.** INV-0013 and INV-0014 both bracket **resurrection, miracles, and divinity _as events_** — neither asserted nor denied, graded at no level, in both directions. That is a scope statement, not a low grade. INV-0011's bracket is worded differently and reaches wider: "theological truth-claims of any tradition." **None of this is contradicted by the Level 2 (Low) that appears in INV-0011's entry below**, which grades a different object entirely — whether Zoroastrian eschatology *shaped* the Jewish doctrinal motifs of resurrection, angelology, and a savior figure. The event is bracketed; the transmission history is graded. Do not read the one as the other.

**INV-0013 — New Testament Texts as Historical Evidence**
Asked: the nature, dating, authorship, and transmission of the earliest sources for Jesus and the first Christian movement, and how strong an evidential base they provide, claim by claim.
Found: a strong but layered and uneven base, and the record's own headline is that **confidence must be assigned by attestation layer, never to "the New Testament" as a whole** — a single figure for the corpus would be a category error. Anchors **Level 4 (High)** (wording recoverability, the undisputed Paulines c. 50–60 CE, the early creedal tradition); reconstructed layers **Level 3 (Moderate)** (precise Gospel dating, non-Christian attestation); traditional apostolic authorship and Q **Level 2 (Low)**. Mythicism is steelmanned and does not survive; the maximalist pole is not sustained either. The record's own methodological self-assessment is §7.6-gated and is not summarized here.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0014 — The Historical Jesus**
Asked: what can be established, and at what evidential grade, about the life, deeds, and teachings of the historical Jesus of Nazareth.
Found: a minimal core at **Level 4 (High)** — existence and the fact of crucifixion, on the contemporary Pauline layer; a ministry profile at **Level 3 (Moderate)** — baptism by John, the Kingdom-of-God proclamation in a Galilean itinerant frame, repute as exorcist and healer; and a falling floor beneath the specifics, with **specific-saying wording at Level 2 (Low)**, where the community-invention rival was steelmanned and **is recorded as winning that item-level component**. No Level 5 anywhere. Both poles fail: too well-anchored for mythicism, too layer-bounded for maximalism.
**This record carries its own reliance gate, and it is not the health gate.** It states it four times: this is a **§7.5-analog externally-consequential** investigation — not health, not child-facing, but adjacent to the owner's comparative-religion thesis — closed on a **verification-split review**, and therefore **"NOT cleared for external reliance (thesis or public use) regardless of this closure."** The gate-lift the record names is **independent re-verification** — a different model against critical editions, or a qualified New Testament historian. See §3.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0011 — Zoroastrian Eschatology and Second Temple Apocalypticism**
Asked: to what extent, and through what evidenced pathways, Zoroastrian eschatology shaped Second Temple Jewish apocalypticism — the stream the earliest Jesus movement inherited.
Found: **a split verdict, and the record states the split is the finding.** Best-supported for cosmic dualism and for individual/final judgment (**Level 3, Moderate**); the transmission of resurrection and of angelology/demonology, together with the Saoshyant savior figure, at **Level 2 (Low)** — with the savior strand leaning negative, so the popular "Persian savior → Jewish messiah → Christ" pipeline is **not supported**. No doctrine reaches established, because one structural fact caps every directional claim: systematic Zoroastrian eschatology is written down centuries after the Jewish texts it is compared with (**Level 4, High**, for that asymmetry). Sustained Persian-period contact is attested and supplies **opportunity, not proof**. The Nazarene inheritance terminus is **Level 4 (High)** and is **explicitly firewalled** — it may not be assembled with the contested doctrine claims into a Persia→Christianity chain.
Closed 2026-07-21.

## 4.3 Tradition origins, lineage, and family classification

*What this cluster answers:* where a tradition came from, whether one tradition descends from another, and how a tradition is placed in a family. **INV-0016, INV-0017, and INV-0018 are the ADR-GOV-0009 world-religions timeline program** and are the only records that create tradition entities and lineage edges. **INV-0015 is adjacent but not part of the program** — it asks how a scholarly field *classifies* a tradition, and it creates no entity.

All four bracket the traditions' truth claims entirely and work in a neutral religious-studies register that adjudicates no tradition's self-account. In the two that put a tradition's own self-understanding **in scope as an object of study** — INV-0015 and INV-0018 — that becomes an explicit **dual-track rule**: the self-account is a fact **about** the tradition, which neither settles the historical question nor is dismissed as error, and neither track may be used to move the other's grade.

**Read together, the three timeline-program records — INV-0016, INV-0017, and INV-0018** — produce a timeline of **two connected clusters plus one deliberately unconnected node**. That picture is the finding, not a gap.

**INV-0016 — Iranian Religious Family Origins and Branching**
Asked: what can be established, and at what grade, about the origins and branching of the Iranian religious family — Zoroastrianism, Zurvanism, Manichaeism, Mazdakism, Yazidism — including each emergence dating and the nature of each descent relationship.
Found: one root (Zoroastrianism), **one internal current, and three branching traditions** — Manichaeism `syncretic-descent`, Mazdakism `heterodox-offshoot`, Yazidism `disputed`. **The headline is an overturn: the scaffold's working table had Zurvanism as a branch, and the evidence made it a heterodox current within Zoroastrianism — no entity, no lineage edge.** The record calls this the distinctness threshold discriminating, which is the threshold working. **No verdict rises above Moderate and three of five components are Level 2 (Low)** — the honest outcome for a family built on extinction, contested dating, and hostile-source-only attestation. Yazidism's `disputed` is a finding, not a hedge.
Closed 2026-07-21.

**INV-0017 — Judaism–Christianity Family Origins and Branching**
Asked: the origins and branching of the Judaism–Christianity family — the emergence of Second Temple Judaism and the descent of both Christianity and Rabbinic Judaism from it (the "parting of the ways"), including the nature of each descent relationship.
Found: one fork — Second Temple Judaism as root carrying **no** lineage edge (the deeper Israelite root deferred, not asserted), with **both edges on the children**: Christianity as a first-century branch (`schism`, **Level 3 Moderate** for the qualifier fit, under a load-bearing caveat that `schism` must **not** be read as a sharp datable break), and Rabbinic Judaism as the post-70-CE **main-line continuation**. The Christianity spine is **Level 4 (High)** but **inherited** from closed prior claims, not derived here.
**A caution for readers, because the record and the current graph differ:** this record's continuation qualifier was graded **Level 2 (Low)** to encode a *vocabulary* failure — the controlled list had no word for main-line continuation and `reform` was used least-wrong under protest. **The Low encodes the strain in the vocabulary, not doubt about the descent.** The owner has since governed that gap (ADR-GOV-0010 added `continuation` and re-qualified the edge), so the record's `reform` reads one step behind the graph. Read the record for the reasoning; read STD-0004 for the current vocabulary.
Closed 2026-07-22.

**INV-0018 — Islam Origins and Abrahamic Relationships**
Asked: what can be established about the emergence and dating of Islam as a tradition, and about the nature of its historical relationship to Judaism and Christianity — descent, influence, or neither.
Found: **a separate root with influence relationships and no descent edge.** Seventh-century CE emergence in western Arabia and the adjoining late-antique Near East is the family's one firm result at **Level 4 (High)**, earned on convergence between the field's mainstream and revisionist poles despite opposed method; confessional crystallisation is a different and genuinely contested question at **Level 3 (Moderate)**; the relationships to Judaism and to Christianity are each **influence and engagement, not descent, at Level 3 (Moderate)**, reasoned independently by different routes.
**Does not reduce, and the reason matters.** Its load-bearing output is a **reasoned refusal**: zero lineage edges, reviewer-verified against six explicit tests, with `disputed` neither used nor usable to hedge for influence. "No edge" here does **not** mean thin evidence or no finding — the record finds substantial documented relationships and refuses only to model them as descent, and it states that an unconnected node is an honest rendering requiring no repair. A short form that says "Islam: no edge" inverts the finding. **Open the record.**
Closed 2026-07-23.

**INV-0015 — Yiguandao Classification** *(adjacent to the program, not part of it — no entity, no edge)*
Asked: how academic religious studies classifies Yiguandao in relation to Daoism and to Chinese salvationist religions, on what evidential basis, at what grade, with what consensus or dissent — and how that relates to Yiguandao's own self-understanding.
**Does not reduce, and the record's own structure is why.** The surveyed anglophone literature places Yiguandao in the salvationist / redemptive-society family with effective unanimity, and no surveyed source classifies it as a school of Daoism (**Level 3, Moderate**). But the *"on what basis"* half is **Level 2 (Low)**, because the field's criteria-mechanics sit behind paywalls and the record **refused to reconstruct them from model memory**, grading down instead. **No Level 4 anywhere** — the verification posture forbids it. Two of six components are a bounded negative and an unreachable interior, which a short form silently converts into positive support. The record additionally holds the owner's own comparative thesis at arm's length as the hypothesis under test — not evidence, not citable, not a premise — and this finding is gated from thesis-supporting use. **Do not cite the headline without both grades and that gate. Open the record.**
Closed 2026-07-20.

## 4.4 Health, stress, and mental-health epidemiology

*What this cluster answers:* what the general evidence supports about a health or behavioural question — never what any individual should do. **Every record here is health/high-stakes class under STD-0006 §7.5 and is gated from client-facing, prospect-facing, and public use in any stream at any confidence level.** Each brackets the individual clinical case explicitly.

**All four record a motivated-reasoning guard, but they do not all name the same hazard, and the difference matters.** **INV-0005 and INV-0006 name the owner's own wellness work:** INV-0006's guard opens by stating that the owner runs a wellness business and that a confident "here is how to make people stick" conclusion would be commercially convenient; INV-0005's names the interventions closest to the owner's wellness-business framing — mindfulness, breathwork — as precisely the ones with the thinnest durability evidence. In both, the convenient conclusion **did not fully survive**, and both record the symmetric guard against over-correcting. **INV-0004 names a cultural and commercial narrative instead** — the "cure without drugs" prior it attributes to the wellness industry and some low-carb advocacy, not to the owner's own work — and reads the evidence deliberately against it. **INV-0010's guard names neither:** it names three convenient tidy stories (the "it's just puberty/biology" reading, the "it's all referral bias, no real difference" over-correction, and any collapse of sex into gender) and resists each.

**This is the cluster where short forms do the most damage. Two of its four records carry the `Does not reduce` marker** — INV-0004 and INV-0006.

**INV-0004 — Metformin Discontinuation and T2D Remission**
Asked: what the evidence says about interventions that improve glycemic control and metabolic function in type 2 diabetes, the possibility and conditions of remission and safely discontinuing metformin, and the factors — including long duration — affecting how likely that is.
**Does not reduce.** The record's own statement is "a real but conditional conclusion," and the three governing caveats are load-bearing, not trimmings: remission likelihood **declines with duration and lost beta-cell reserve**; **remission is not cure** and relapses in a substantial majority over years; and **metformin must never be stopped unilaterally** — de-prescribing is physician-supervised. Confidence is split and unmerged: **Level 4 (High)** for existence and mechanism, **Level 3 (Moderate)** for the overall practical answer. A short form keeping "weight loss can put T2D into remission" and dropping the caveats reproduces exactly the "cure without drugs" narrative the record brackets against. **Not individualized medical advice. Open the record.**
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0005 — Chronic Stress Interventions**
Asked: which intervention classes have evidence of **durable** (>6–12 month) relief from chronic stress in general adult populations, graded separately for short-term and long-term effect.
Found, negative first: **no single intervention has strong (>1-year) durability evidence.** The most defensible durable strategy is a combination — lifestyle foundations (with CBT-I the best-evidenced durable skill), response-regulation skills for reliable short-to-medium-term relief, and, **on mechanistic grounds** (the record deliberately softened this from "decisively"), acting on the stressor itself where it is controllable. Techniques that regulate only the response have **thin evidence of durable relief**. Synthesis **Level 3 (Moderate)**, capped at its weakest link and explicitly not reaching High. Do not signpost this as "stress techniques work."
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0006 — Wellness Intervention Adherence and Maintenance**
Asked: the most common factors behind failure to adopt or maintain wellness interventions, and the interventions with the strongest evidence against those factors.
**Does not reduce, and the record proves it:** an earlier declarative short form was **overturned in review** as inconsistent with its own Moderate body. The diagnosis is the stronger half — the failure is not lack of desire but the **intention–behaviour gap** (**Level 4, High**). The prescriptive half is weaker (**Level 3, Moderate**): the best-evidenced levers target translation and automatization rather than exhortation, with **modest effects**, and **>12-month maintenance evidence is thin for every technique**. The record's own bottom line is that **there is no reliable formula**, and it carries a useful negative: financial incentives work while in place and fade on removal. Do not present the two halves at equal strength.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0010 — Psychosocial Stressor Onset Divergence by Sex or Gender, Ages 5–17**
Asked: where the evidence documents divergence by sex or gender in the **onset timing** of recognized psychosocial stressors in ages 5–17 — and, for each, **what construct was actually measured: sex, gender, or unresolved.**
Found: five documented onset-timing loci with distinct signatures (depression at the pubertal transition **Level 4 (High)**, timing only; conduct, ADHD identification, and eating-disorder consolidation **Level 3 (Moderate)**; anxiety **Level 2 (Low)** rising to Moderate for direction). **The headline is the construct answer: seven of the eight sources measure neither construct cleanly — five conflated, two unresolved, one sex-measured, none gender-measured — so "sex differences," in this corpus, is largely a labeling convention over construct-unresolved data.** Explicitly **not** supported: biological-sex causation of any divergence, a hormonal cause even for depression, any ranking by magnitude, and any reading of the ADHD divergence as a real rate difference. The census is robust for its eight sources and not a population estimate.
**Two firewalls travel with this record.** Its §7.5 gate was declared **at opening, before the evidence**, so that no interim state existed in which a finding looked available. And the record **binds a non-inference: it does not unpark, unblock, or bear on children's programming, and a reader must not assemble it with INV-0008 into a permission neither grants.** That non-inference is stated on INV-0008's entry as well as here.
Closed 2026-07-15.

## 4.5 Development, learning, and childhood

*What this cluster answers:* what is actually demonstrated about how children develop competence in structured, participatory, or mentored settings — and how far those findings survive translation to a different setting. **Both records are child-facing high-stakes class under STD-0006 §7.5 and gated absolutely from client-facing or external use pending re-verification outside Relatio's circuit.** Both are **dual-graded**: what the underlying literature supports is graded separately from how far it transfers to the proposed application, and **neither collapses into a single number.** Both carry the `Does not reduce` marker, and in both cases the dual grade is the reason.

**INV-0008 — Skills-Based Child Development Programming**
Asked: what the literature supports about structured, adult-mentored, skills-based programming (heritage skills, outdoor settings, hands-on craft) as a context for healthy child development — and at what confidence, per outcome domain.
**Does not reduce; the per-domain table is the honest representation and the record says so.** The ingredients are individually supported **unevenly**: skill mastery → self-efficacy and structured non-parental mentoring at **Level 4 (High)** underlying, with mentoring's average benefit **small** (d ≈ 0.21) and conditional; nature, risky play, and co-regulation **Level 3 (Moderate)** and thinner or shorter-term; family routines the weakest strand at **Level 2 (Low)** on confounded cross-sectional data. And the compound: **no located study evaluates the bundle as one integrated intervention (Level 4, High, that the gap exists)** — which holds the *application* grades far beneath the underlying ones. Five of the six domains sit at **Level 2 (Low)** or **Level 1 (Very Low)** for application; structured mentoring alone reaches **Level 3 (Moderate)**; and the finding's own compound-application component is **Level 2 (Low)**. The record's posture is **build-and-evaluate, not build-and-assume**, and it asks that framing be quoted verbatim if cited. Also not supported: any specific value of heritage or craft content over generic structured skill, and durability.
**A non-inference binds this record to INV-0010, and it is stated on both sides.** INV-0010 does not unpark, unblock, or bear on children's programming; **this record's own gate remains that precondition**, and a reader must not assemble the two into a permission neither grants.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0009 — Learning by Observing and Pitching In (LOPI)**
Asked: what is actually demonstrated about children developing competence through participation in real family and community activity, and how far those findings survive translation to a single-child home-education setting.
**Does not reduce, because the finding is an asymmetry that needs both grades to parse.** In the studied communities children develop genuine competence substantially through observing and contributing (**Level 3, Moderate**), carried by mechanisms of which shared attention is best-evidenced. Transfer to a single-child home is **Level 1 (Very Low)**, rising to **Level 2 (Low)** for one facet, and is **capped independently of the base grade**: the community-organization and mixed-age facets have no substrate in a single-child home and **no located study tests LOPI in that setting.** The record's blunt statement is that **the part of LOPI with the best science is exactly the part a solitary learner cannot get.** It is not a validation of any home-education method, and not a finding that instruction is unnecessary — LOPI explicitly includes guidance.
Closed 2026-07-20.

## 4.6 Complex and natural systems

*What this cluster answers:* how a mechanism in a physical or biological system actually works, and how far the evidence carries past the mechanism itself.

Both records establish a descriptive mechanism at **Level 4 (High)** and both stop deliberately short of Level 5 — but they stop for **different reasons, and the difference is the point.** **INV-0002's question is mechanistic and empirical, not interpretive**, and its limits are scope and precision: the bottleneck-free case, approximate thresholds and wave speeds. There is no popular narrative standing in front of it and no asymmetry to report. **INV-0012 is the record where a popular narrative has grown up around the biology** — and there the gap between a well-evidenced descriptive layer and an over-reaching interpretive one is the finding, not a hedge.

**INV-0002 — Phantom Traffic Jams**
Asked: why traffic jams form on highways with no bottleneck, accident, or obstruction; what the mechanism is, and how well established it is.
Found: above a critical density, free-flowing traffic becomes **dynamically unstable**, and ordinary reaction-time delays amplify small perturbations into **self-sustaining stop-and-go waves that propagate backward** — an emergent property of the collective system, not of any vehicle or external cause. **Level 4 (High)**, on an independent controlled experiment and convergent modeling; held below Level 5 for model-choice openness and controlled-track idealization. Wave-speed figures are approximate (direction Level 4, exact speed Level 3), and the result concerns the bottleneck-free case — real congestion often combines both.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0012 — Mycelial Networks and Fungal Cognition**
Asked: how mycelial networks grow, transport resources, and signal internally — and, graded against a defined ladder of cognition, what the evidence warrants attributing to them and what it does not.
**Does not reduce: stating either half alone inverts the finding.** The two halves must be read together. Filamentous fungi **are** genuinely sophisticated self-organizing adaptive networks — resource-directed foraging and source–sink-regulated bidirectional transport at **Level 4 (High)**, whole-colony integration from local rules with no central controller at **Level 3 (Moderate)** — biology even deflationary experts accept. But the cognitive interpretation is weak: memory-like carry-over is real and **operational only**, resting on a single unreplicated study (**Level 2, Low**); basal cognition is **Level 2 (Low)** and only as a contested framing, so the evidence licenses *"arguably a candidate for basal cognition,"* never *"is cognitive"*; and **sentience is bracketed as undetermined — neither asserted nor denied, graded at no level.** Three popular claims are specifically **not** supported: fungal "language" (**Level 1, Very Low** for the language reading), the strong wood-wide web (kin-provisioning has no peer-reviewed field support), and the fungal brain.
Closed 2026-07-20.

## 4.7 Knowledge systems

*What this cluster answers:* what outside literatures and outside systems document about designing and operating structured knowledge work — how formal constraints behave, and how established systems handle recurring design problems.

**Both records bear on knowledge systems and both carry a §7.6-gated reflexive component. Those components are not described here, and neither investigation may be presented — anywhere — as validating this vault's own design.** What follows is each record's finding about its **external** subject matter only.

**INV-0007 — Formal Constraints in Knowledge Systems**
Asked: what the knowledge-management, research-methodology, and organizational/epistemic-design literatures say about the **value** and the **failure modes** of formal constraints in evolving knowledge systems.
**Does not reduce: the entire content is in the qualifiers.** The literatures license neither "constraints are good" nor "bad" but a conditional — constraints are **valuable but failure-prone**, and whether they help depends on **enabling, fitted, and revisable** design rather than primarily on their amount. Value is real and in one case quantified: undisclosed analytic flexibility drove a false-positive rate to 61% against a nominal 5%, with disclosure and protocol constraints the demonstrated remedy (**Level 4, High** — though that number is a simulation of capacity, not a measured base rate). Four failure modes recur: **decoupling / ceremonial compliance, gaming and goal displacement, ossification, and the context-stripping cost of over-codifying tacit knowledge** — the first two sharpest for evolving systems. The record states plainly that these are **design conditions associated with success, not a validated recipe**, carries the counterweight that much valuable standardization is deliberately uniform and non-participatory, notes that the synthesis is itself gameable, and grades the whole **Level 3 (Moderate)**. Its key limitation is transfer by analogy from workplace procedure, organizational learning, and commons research. The predictable short form — "constraints work when well designed" — is the self-flattering reading the record names and guards against.
Closed 2026-07-20.

**INV-0019 — Structured-Knowledge Systems Comparative Survey**
Asked: across established structured-knowledge systems in argumentation modelling, evidence synthesis, semantic publishing, and professional citation practice — how each individuates a claim, serves expert and lay readers from one record, makes the source-to-claim path recoverable, and warrants its typed relationships; what empirical evidence exists that these approaches perform as designed; and what is documented about where they break down and what they cost to operate.
**Does not reduce, and the negative is the part most easily misread.** On its nine-source base the surveyed systems answer these problems chiefly **by design rather than by demonstrated performance**: the approaches are articulate and the rationales explicit, but **measured evidence is confined to exactly two studies, and both found deployed practice falling far short of its design** — a plain-language-summary adherence study (1738 summaries analysed, not one fully adherent, 57% overall, and the certainty item — the register's epistemic core — the least-adhered at 0.7%) and a citator study (substantive three-way agreement on 11% of examined relationships, with individual failure rates on clearly negative treatments, every mapping rendered as unqualified fact). Everywhere else, evidence of performance is **explicitly absent**. No surveyed system states an operational claim-splitting rule or enforces one by tooling, and **typing is an assertion in every surveyed system — none requires or tool-checks a warrant.** All four pictures are **Level 3 (Moderate)**; there is **no Level 4 in the finding and no Level 5 anywhere**, with one **Level 2 (Low)** component for two interiors that could not be read and were disclosed rather than compensated.
**A caveat travels with the 0.7% and must not be dropped.** The record registers an **unadjudicated cross-source divergence about the instrument the figure was measured against**: the adherence study describes the standard as containing 14 items (12 mandatory), while the archived v3.0 standard itself contains 12 (10 mandatory, 2 highly desirable). The record files this as a divergence between sources, **declines to adjudicate it** — an explicit rejection, not an oversight — and names it as one reason the approach-description component is held at Level 4 rather than Level 5. Quote the figure only with the divergence attached.
Three further bounds are load-bearing and travel with any use: **absence claims are within-base only** (nine sources do not represent four fields; whether measurements exist elsewhere was not surveyed); **neither empirical study generalizes beyond its own subjects**, and the register picture is **single-domain by construction**; and the survey **ranks, adjudicates, and recommends nothing.** Its two quotable figures are the most dangerous content in it.
Closed 2026-07-24.

# 5. How to use this

**If your question looks like one of the above:** open that record. Read its own §1 for what was actually asked (often narrower than the cluster heading), its confidence components as they stand, and its brackets and negatives — which in this corpus are frequently the most valuable part. Then check §3 of this document before doing anything outward-facing with it.

**If a record's headline seems to answer your question in one line:** be suspicious, and look for the bolded **`Does not reduce`** marker at the head of that record's entry. **Nine of the nineteen carry it.** The marker means one specific thing: a short form of that record is a **known misreading rather than a rough approximation** — it inverts the finding, or silently converts a bounded negative or an unreachable interior into positive support. Entries without the marker are not thereby safe to compress; they are only ones where a rough summary is rough rather than wrong.

**If your question is not covered here:** that is worth knowing, and it is the honest answer this map exists to give. An uncovered question is a **candidate for a new investigation**, to be raised with the owner — who decides whether and when one opens. There is no intake procedure to follow yet; that object does not exist.

**If this map and a record disagree:** the record wins, and the divergence is worth reporting.
