# FND-0021

# Reading Performance Evidence Synthesis

## Draft Finding Record

---

# 1. Statement

> **Within this sixteen-source base, the measured/asserted boundary in reading-performance design runs not between territories but between DIRECTIONS and VALUES: the measured regions are real — several were read this session — yet in every one of the six territories the prescriptions' specific values fail to trace to reading measurement at the surfaces accessible this session.** Specifically: **(1)** typeface and letterform prescriptions state specific values (point sizes, 120–145% line spacing, 45–90 characters) from professional custom while the serif question's one in-base measurement reports reading speeds unaffected by serifs (RSVP and continuous reading) with only a small size-threshold advantage for slight serifs that the study attributes to letter spacing, the reported sans-serif preference stands only at citing-literature level, and the research lineage's own doctoral critique reports no valid conclusion in either direction; **(2)** the WCAG 2.x contrast thresholds — the territory's dominant prescription — descend by their own read derivation chain from display-ergonomics standards times a clinical acuity adjustment, not from reading measurement, while the read reading psychophysics shows fluent-size reading tolerating tenfold contrast reduction and collapsing at small sizes, a size-contrast interaction no blanket ratio expresses, and the candidate successor's read critique documentation cites no peer-reviewed validation of its own thresholds; **(3)** hue is a measured near-non-factor for normal-vision reading given adequate contrast of either kind (two convergent programs, one read), with a read low-vision reversal — and the hue-for-reading prescription pole is itself thin; **(4)** the aging-vision territory is the base's genuinely strongest measured region — the read anchor documents convergent, mechanism-attributed contrast-sensitivity, low-luminance, dark-adaptation, and processing-speed declines — and its derived prescriptions match the measured DIRECTION while their specific values (16–18 pt; 25–30% leading) are uncited in the read artifact and the brief's named blue-region sub-territory is absent from the base; **(5)** the read media meta-analysis measures a real paper-over-screen comprehension advantage (g = −.21; time-pressure and genre moderated; increasing 2000–2017; not age-moderated) while the medium-specific size prescriptions connect to none of it at accessible surfaces; **(6)** small-screen prescriptions are the cleanest case of assertion — a per-platform size table with zero cited studies on the read page — beside a narrow measured leg (closer-than-assumed viewing distances, at abstract level) and an in-base absence of any direct small-screen reading-performance measurement.** The prescriptions outrun the measurements in all six territories at the VALUE level; in two territories (size/contrast and aging vision) the measured base is strong and read, and there the honest formulation is not "unmeasured" but "measured — and the prescribed numbers still do not come from the measurements." Everything is within-base, R0, and application-free.

# 4. Scope & Limitations

- **Within-base only.** Sixteen sources do not represent these literatures; every absence claim binds surfaces accessible this session, with unread interiors disclosed per claim. Known base omissions (recorded, not filled): the ISO 9241-3 / ANSI-HFES interior evidence bases behind WCAG's 3:1; the blue-region/lens-yellowing aging literature; small-screen eye-tracking and performance studies; post-2017 medium studies; children's typeface/legibility research; non-anglophone literatures.
- **Domain-general; application-free.** No organization, brand, product, or application decision appears anywhere; use in any applied stream requires the crosswalk and is gated (R0).
- **The boundary formulation is descriptive, not a verdict on the prescriptions' wisdom:** values can be reasonable without tracing to measurement; the finding locates provenance, not merit.
- **Era-boundedness is real and disclosed:** the read psychophysics primaries are 1987–1991 CRT-era work; the read review (2011) and meta (2018) are newer; the medium corpus ends 2017.
- **The INV-0020 boundary held:** no response/emotion/association claim is made or re-litigated anywhere in this record.
