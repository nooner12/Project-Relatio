## 3. Findings

### Determinate flags (must be remediated; I do not rewrite the records)

**F1 — Quotation-fidelity failure in CLM-0111 element (iii) (population sub-element).** *Criterion: KOS-0003 §12.1(4) (source fidelity; misquotation is misrepresentation of the source's words) / STD-0006 §5.7.* Defective text: `"We found no advantages of color contrast for low-vision reading. For text composed of 60' characters, all low-vision subjects read better with luminance contrast than with color contrast."` — presented as verbatim from a read interior. **The source says "6° characters," not "60' characters"** (verified this session against BOTH the PubMed abstract, which renders "6 degrees characters," and the laboratory-hosted PDF, whose extracted text renders the degree glyph as "60 characters" — the likely mechanical origin of the error: the extraction's degree sign collapsed into a zero and was then mis-repaired as 60 arcminutes). 60′ = 1°; the source's figure is 6° — a factor-of-six error in a quoted quantity. The substance survives — 6° characters are exactly the "large text" the claim statement asserts, and the first quoted sentence is verbatim-exact — which is why this is Flagged, not Blocked. But the record's distinguishing strength for this source is that the abstract and conclusions were "extracted verbatim," and this quotation is not verbatim. *Remediation that satisfies me:* correct the quoted figure to the source's 6° (in whatever notation the record adopts, saying which surface renders it how), and re-check the "extracted verbatim" label on CLM-0111's Verification disclosure against the corrected text. No grade movement required.

**F2 — SRC-0180's representation in CLM-0109 misstates its establishing surface and under-reports its abstract (propagates to INV-0021 §4 and FND-0021 statement (1)).** *Criterion: INV-0021's own §3.2 rule (a source whose interior was not read supports only what its abstract, metadata, or the citing literature actually establishes, **and the record says which**) / STD-0006 §7.5 (a false access disclosure is the same failure class as silence) / KOS-0003 §12.1(2).* Three defects in one source's representation:

*Remediation that satisfies me:* relabel the preference leg to its actual establishing surface (naming the citing-literature basis, or dropping the dissociation to the strength that surface supports), correct the presentation-mode wording to the abstract's, add the 5%-serif threshold result (with the authors' spacing attribution and the abstract's concluding sentence, so the null bottom line is carried fairly), and propagate the corrected access labeling to INV-0021 §4 and FND-0021 statement (1). **No grade movement is required if remediated as specified** — (iii) Moderate and (iv) Low each survive on the corrected basis; if the "abstract-establishable" labeling were retained against this flag, (iii) and (iv) should drop a level for resting on an unread interior without saying so.
