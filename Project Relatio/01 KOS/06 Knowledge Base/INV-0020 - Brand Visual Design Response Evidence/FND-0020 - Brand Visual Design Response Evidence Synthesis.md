# FND-0020

# Brand Visual Design Response Evidence Synthesis

## Draft Finding Record

---

# 1. Statement

> **Within this eighteen-source base, the prescriptive practices of brand visual design outrun their measured psychological and physiological response evidence in all six regions examined — and the measured regions that do exist are non-prescriptive in character.** Specifically: **(1)** the single-hue code rests on measured color-emotion ASSOCIATIONS (partly universal at the association level, nationally modulated, developmentally emergent) while its response evidence is small, context-dependent, adversely replicated, or absent, and the field's own principal review states that application recommendations are not yet warranted; **(2)** color-combination and proportion rules have NO measured response evidence in this base at the access achieved — the nearest measured work is single-color preference, and even the prescriptions' canonical statements could not be sourced at any surface accessible this session (the practitioner manual's unread interior being the disclosed hole those absence claims do not cover); **(3)** typeface character and congruence is the base's most measured region, with peer-reviewed impression and congruity effects matching the prescription's shape — but at abstract-level access, single studies, perception outcomes, and no in-base replication; **(4)** mark-shape geometry (roundness/angularity) is genuinely measured at the same access level, while symbolic-content claims beyond geometry have no measured base here at all; **(5)** placement rules stratify cleanly into measured gaze allocation, visible-but-unmeasured extrapolation to effectiveness, and a golden-ratio stratum whose peer-reviewed examination is scoped to "false or seriously misleading" claims; **(6)** physiological prescriptions were the least sourceable family at the accessible surfaces, the best measured autonomic result runs in a direction the measuring source itself calls PARADOXICAL, and **no source in this base measures any physiological response to a brand asset — the environmental-to-brand transfer step is uniformly unmeasured.** Where the replication record exists it is adverse or contested (near-zero preregistered replications, a documented decline effect, upward-bias indications, an adversarial collaboration ending at odds). Everything is within-base, R0, and application-free.

# 2. Supporting Claims

- **CLM-0103** (single-hue) — the association-vs-response dissolution and the read replication record.
- **CLM-0104** (combination/harmony) — the pre-authorized empty (iii), found emptier than anticipated (even the (i) pole thin).
- **CLM-0105** (typeface) — the strongest measured region; calibration anchor; graded flat by access limits, flagged.
- **CLM-0106** (mark shape) — measured geometry vs unmeasured symbolism, kept structurally separate.
- **CLM-0107** (placement) — the three strata, with the extrapolation step located inside the origin artifact.
- **CLM-0108** (physiological) — the direction paradox, lab-vs-field divergence, and the unmeasured transfer step.

# 3. Confidence (KOS-0003 §8)

Eight components, native Level N (Label), never averaged; the synthesis never exceeds its weakest necessary claim:

- Six per-region pictures — **Level 3 (Moderate)** each, inheriting their claims' (iii) grades and bounded by the same access limits.
- **replication_record_adversity — Level 3 (Moderate):** the adverse record (decline effect, preregistered near-zero subset, bias indications, unresolved adversarial collaboration) rests on a fully read meta-analysis but binds one effect family in depth; most cited effects have no replication information in this base, which is recorded as absence, not safety.
- **unread_interior_coverage — Level 2 (Low):** fourteen of eighteen interiors were not read (paywalled, scan-blocked, or book-form); what this synthesis can say about their contents is bounded accordingly — the binding weakest component, deliberately carried at the finding level as in FND-0019.
- **No Level 5 and no Level 4 anywhere** — consistent with the brief's pre-authorized outcome class; the finding declines any grade its weakest necessary components cannot carry.

# 4. Scope & Limitations

- **Within-base only.** Eighteen sources do not represent six literatures; every absence claim binds this base, not the field. The base's known omissions include (recorded, not filled): the blue/red cognitive-performance literature and its replication record; peer-reviewed scan-path replications; combination-preference measurement; any second physiological program; non-anglophone literatures.
- **Domain-general; application-free.** No organization, brand, product, or application decision appears anywhere; use of these findings in any applied stream requires the crosswalk and is gated (R0).
- **Access-bounded.** Four interiors read (SRC-0165, SRC-0172, SRC-0176, SRC-0178), one abstract retrieved verbatim (SRC-0161); everything else surface-level, disclosed per claim.
- **The replication-crisis posture cuts both ways and is recorded doing so:** the adverse record is specific to the one deeply-studied family; the absence of replication information elsewhere is an unknown, not an indictment — and not a reassurance.
- **Population and cultural variation** are recorded as sub-elements where the base supports them (cultural and developmental within CLM-0103) and as explicit absences elsewhere (CLM-0105, CLM-0106).
