# CLM-0107

# Placement and Layout Attention Claims (CLM-E)

## Draft Claim Record

---

# Claim
> **The placement/layout prescription family separates cleanly into the three mandated strata within this base. Stratum 1 (measured attention findings): a genuine eye-tracking attention literature exists (a monograph-length academic review), and the F-pattern scan observation is eye-tracking-derived (232 users), with the originating article itself calling the pattern "a rough, general shape rather than a uniform, pixel-perfect behavior." Stratum 2 (plausible extrapolations): the layout rules derived from scan observations — front-load the first two paragraphs, lead with information-carrying words — are prescriptions the originating article derives from gaze data without measuring comprehension or response outcomes; the extrapolation step is visible in the read artifact itself. Stratum 3 (folklore): the golden-ratio/divine-proportion composition family lands where expected — a peer-reviewed debunking literature documents its claims in art, architecture, and aesthetics as false or seriously misleading, verified here at the strength the accessible surface supports rather than assumed.**

---

# Element (i) — The Prescriptive Approach (as stated in the practice literature)

- **Scan-pattern rules (read directly, SRC-0172):** the originating F-pattern article itself prescribes: users "won't read your text thoroughly in a word-by-word manner"; place the most important information in "the first two paragraphs"; "start subheads, paragraphs, and bullet points with information-carrying words." The scan-pattern prescription family is thus documented from its origin artifact, live-read.
- **Position-of-prominence and golden-ratio composition rules:** no primary practitioner artifact in this base states them; the golden-ratio family's circulation is documented via the debunking literature's existence and stated scope (SRC-0173's abstract: claims about the golden ratio in art, architecture, literature, and aesthetics that are "false or seriously misleading" — establishing that such claims circulate). Recorded as an indirect (i) pole for that portion.

# Element (ii) — The Stated Rationale

- Scan-pattern rules: attention is systematically and measurably distributed, so layout should follow measured gaze allocation (the read article's implicit rationale, visible in its derivation of rules from eyetracking heatmaps).
- Golden-ratio composition: the circulating rationale (an inherently pleasing proportion) is documented only via the debunking literature's characterization at surface level; no primary statement is in base.

# Element (iii) — Measured Psychological/Physiological Response Evidence (including replication status and explicit absence) — THE THREE STRATA

**Stratum 1 — measured attention findings.**
- An academic eye-tracking-in-marketing literature exists at monograph scale: SRC-0171 (interior unread; publisher-surface establishable) reviews visual-attention theory and eye-movement research as applied to marketing stimuli. Its existence establishes the stratum; its specific findings are not asserted (unread interior).
- The F-pattern observation (SRC-0172, **read live this session**): eyetracking recordings of 232 users across thousands of pages; a dominant scan shape of two horizontal stripes then a vertical stripe; **with the article's own qualifications**: "The F viewing pattern is a rough, general shape rather than a uniform, pixel-perfect behavior"; "users' scan patterns are not always comprised of exactly three parts" (E and inverted-L variants named). Practitioner-research provenance and commercial stake are in the catalog record. **Replication status: a 2017 follow-up by the same organization revisits and qualifies the pattern (same-team, not independent); no independent peer-reviewed replication is in this base — stated.**

**Stratum 2 — plausible extrapolations from measured attention.**
- The read artifact derives layout **prescriptions** (front-loading; information-carrying words) from gaze observations. **What was measured is gaze allocation; what is prescribed concerns effective communication. The article does not measure comprehension, persuasion, or any downstream response outcome of following its rules** — the extrapolation step from "users look here" to "put your message here and it will work better" is visible in the artifact and unmeasured within it. Recorded as the stratum's defining shape, from the read source itself.

**Stratum 3 — folklore with no citation trail.**
- The golden-ratio/divine-proportion composition family: SRC-0173 (interior is a page-image scan with **no text layer**; two hosted mirrors tried; abstract/surface-establishable only) is a peer-reviewed mathematics-journal examination whose stated scope is that much of what is presented about the golden ratio in art, architecture, and aesthetics is **"false or seriously misleading."** The expected third-stratum placement is thus **verified at the strength the accessible surface supports**: a peer-reviewed debunking exists whose stated subject is precisely this claim family's evidential failure. Page-level specifics (the Parthenon, Great Pyramid, and aesthetic-preference experiment critiques) are **not asserted** (unread interior). **A documented dispute is noted:** a golden-ratio advocacy website publishes a rebuttal of the debunking paper — recorded as evidence the dispute is live in the advocacy literature, not adjudicated. **No measured aesthetic-response evidence for golden-ratio composition exists in this base — explicit absence.**

**EXPLICIT ABSENCE across the element:** no source in this base measures a **response outcome** (comprehension, preference, persuasion, behavior) of following any placement rule. The measured stratum is gaze allocation; everything downstream is extrapolated or asserted.

# Element (iv) — Documented Costs and Failure Modes (from the sources; not inferred)

- **Misapplication of the F-pattern is documented by its own originator:** the same organization's 2017 follow-up is titled to the point — the pattern is "Misunderstood, But Still Relevant" — documenting at surface level that the prescription family is misapplied in practice. (Surface-level: the follow-up's existence and title; its interior was not read.)
- **EXPLICITLY EMPTY beyond that** at the surfaces read. Costs of golden-ratio composition practice are documented nowhere in this base.

# Assumptions (KOS-0003 §10)
- **Gaze ≠ effectiveness (binding for stratum 2).** Attention allocation is not communication success; the extrapolation is recorded as extrapolation.
- **The golden-ratio verification is only as strong as its accessible surface** — the abstract-level scope statement; the interior's specific demolitions are not asserted.
- **The reading-performance/legibility boundary:** SRC-0171's territory touches reading behavior; only attention-allocation content is used here, and reading-performance material is left to the queued legibility investigation (boundary note recorded).

# Reasoning (KOS-0003 §7)
**Descriptive stratification.** Risks checked: (1) **stratum bleed** — letting the measured stratum's credibility launder the extrapolated rules; controlled by locating the extrapolation step inside the read artifact and naming what it does not measure; (2) **debunking-by-title** — asserting Markowsky's specific findings without the interior; controlled by confining stratum 3 to the abstract-level scope statement and disclosing the scan-access failure; (3) **single-organization dependence** — the F-pattern stratum rests on one consultancy's research program; named.
